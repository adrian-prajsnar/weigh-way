### Bug fixes

* Fixed sign-up to correctly let you know if an account already exists with your details.
* Reset the date range in Compare when the age-year view is no longer available, and ensured default week and month ranges start cleanly on period boundaries.
* Made sure you are asked to confirm when saving an unchanged weight, and stopped the edit dialog from refocusing unexpectedly during background refreshes.
* Updated all screen text immediately after changing your language preference.
* Resolved an issue on iOS where confirmation dialogs could overlap with actions.
* Improved Profile to hide your weigh-in count until entries finish loading, and made sure your sex selection reverts properly if saving changes fails.
* Clearer error messages throughout the app so you see specific alerts when saving, deleting, or loading your weigh-ins and profile details, instead of generic messages.
