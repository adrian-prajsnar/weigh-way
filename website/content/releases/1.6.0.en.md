### What's new

* Compare and History now use a single date range picker, and custom comparison periods are shown in chronological order.

### Improvements

* Sign-in, sign-out, and email links for account confirmation, password reset, and Google sign-in work more reliably.
* Offline handling and exporting your weigh-ins are more dependable.
* Numbers in Polish use a decimal comma, and confirmation dialogs work better with screen readers.

### Bug fixes

* The bottom navigation bar no longer covers screen content.
* The weigh-in editor now opens from the top of the screen.
* Fixed calculations around the change of day, age, comparisons, units, and BMI.
* Fixed cases where data could look out of date or a profile field could be lost after saving.
